# ru_maxrss anomaly — resolution note

## Observation
`os.wait4` `ru_maxrss` reported byte-identical peaks for different shells
(earlier run: 15572 KiB for bash and brush across all samples), while live
`psutil` sampling of held processes showed a real spread (bash 2688–2724,
brush ~7044–7080, dash ~2728, fish ~10300 KiB).

## Reproducer
`/tmp/opencode/rusprobe.py`: 3x wait4 on `<sh> -c exit` (maxrss/minflt/majflt)
plus one sleep-held psutil RSS per shell.

Result (linux-cachyos 7.3.0, 2026-10-29):

| shell | wait4 maxrss x3 | minflt | live RSS |
|---|---|---|---|
| bash | 15940 x3 | 167–170 | 2708 |
| brush | 15940 x3 | 314–320 | 7080 |
| dash | 15940 x3 | 106–107 | 2728 |
| fish | 30780–30880 (varies) | ~2420 | 10308 |

## Mechanism (settled by `/tmp/opencode/rusettle.py`)
Two candidate mechanisms were on the table:

- (a) Lane theory: wait4 reports a cumulative `RUSAGE_CHILDREN` monotonic max,
  so a common leaf peak dominates every report. REFUTED: reaping a 60 MB
  python child (maxrss 71476) followed by `dash -c exit` reports 12680 for
  dash, not >= 71476. wait4 is per-child, not cumulative (the lane confused
  it with getrusage(RUSAGE_CHILDREN); its probes were python-only forks, a
  different experiment).
- (b) Tick-granularity plateau: hiwater_rss refreshes at scheduler-tick
  granularity, so sub-tick processes report a stale/quantized value.
  SUPPORTED: fault counts prove the executions differ (bash 167 vs brush 314
  vs dash 106 minflt) yet maxrss ties exactly; the plateau itself drifts
  between runs (15572, then 15940, then dash 12680); longer-lived fish
  (~20-30 ms, several ticks) reports true varying peaks. Exact kernel path
  unverified (Phase-3 eBPF territory, not worth it).

## Impact on the shell verdict
None. All published rankings use live psutil RSS plus wall-clock timing.
Rule: never use wait4/getrusage maxrss for processes living under ~1 tick;
hold with sleep and sample live instead.
